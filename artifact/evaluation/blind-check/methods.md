# Fresh blind AI semantic review

This is a fresh GPT-6 Astra AI review of 48 query–candidate pairs (6 queries × 8 candidates). The model designation is the assigned review-agent configuration, not an independent model-identity measurement. The only source read was `blind_packets.json`; no original pair labels, paper files, historical scores, sibling evidence files, web sources, or external models were consulted. The supplied query, expected answer, and original 0–3 rubric were visible. Thus this is blindness to prior candidate judgments, not blindness to the answer key. No agreement target was used.

Each candidate was judged individually using its canonical/original statement, scope, and provided page excerpts. A candidate received credit for evidence actually present in the packet, including supporting excerpts beyond its canonical sentence. Expected-answer facts were not imputed to candidates merely because they came from the same paper. Relevance follows each query's rubric: useful background can receive 1 where the rubric permits it; unrelated quantities receive 0 where specified. A correct complete F2 response is present in two separate candidates through their supplied supporting excerpts.

`scope_error=true` is reserved for an explicit incompatibility in the support of the same requested result that would substitute a different scoped estimate: here, regional crustal-thickness estimates from outside Afar and an annual regional N-loss estimate for the Peruvian anammox system. Merely sharing terminology, discussing another physical quantity, or providing background does not meet that criterion. Clearly identified different-quantity/background candidates use `scope_assessable=true, scope_error=false`; this means an assessable absence of the narrowly defined substitution error, not proof that all query conditions match. Unresolvable conditions would require `scope_assessable=false, scope_error=null`. No pair required that abstention because the supplied statements/scopes identified the result sufficiently to distinguish these cases; lack of a target answer is not itself a scope-unknown condition.

Evidence limitations: excerpts are often truncated or empty, sometimes contain OCR fragments, and figures/tables are generally captions rather than visible plots/cells. The review cannot verify omitted content or reconstruct numbers from absent figures. For example, q16 candidate 7 supports a qualitative crustal-thickness/velocity association but supplies no thickness values; q30 candidate 4 lists sortable silt in scope but the supplied substantive excerpts concern isotope/productivity results; q70 candidate 5 supplies mostly mixing-plot captions; q110 candidate 2 identifies a crisis interval without its numeric duration. None of these omissions was filled from the expected answer. The two F2 candidates contain the necessary values and method comparison in readable excerpts despite one ending in a truncated sentence.

These are AI semantic judgments, not domain-expert human annotations, independent scientific verification, or scientific ground truth. The outputs are suitable for a separately disclosed AI-review sensitivity check, with the packet's evidence and rubric limitations retained.
