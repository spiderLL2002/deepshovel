# Stored record-weighted abundance and relative-shape descriptions

Existing already-exposed data: deterministic presentation and reviewer QC only; post-exposure descriptive work, not new validation, inference, calibration, fitting, or changed eligibility/weighting.

A is the mean of the four shale-normalized molar concentrations; s is the slope after dividing the weighted mean composition by its A. The listed s is s_of_mean_composition, not the mean of bottle-level s. Alpha=A*s is an algebraic identity, not a causal partition. Geographical support differs between legs and changes between layers. These post-exposure descriptions do not isolate abundance, mixing or fractionation mechanisms.
